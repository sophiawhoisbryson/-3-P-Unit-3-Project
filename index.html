Brownies

  • Time: approx 50-55 min
  • Serves: approx 16 people
Ingredients Quantity
Granated sugar 2 cups
Unswetended cocoa powder 3/4 cup
Large eggs 3
Vanilla extract 1 tbs
Salt 1 tsp
All-purpose flour 1 cup
Semisweet chocolate chips 1.5 cup
Baking powder 1/4 tsp

Steps!

Step 1: Heat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease an 8-inch square baking pan.

Step 2: Whisk together the melted butter, white sugar, eggs, and vanilla extract.

Step 3: Stir in the cocoa powder, all-purpose flour, salt, and baking powder until just combined.

Step 4: Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake for 25 to 30 minutes.

Step 5: Cut into 16 equal squares and enjoy!

Cheese pizza

  • Time: approx 25 min
  • Serves: approx 2 to 3 people
Ingredients Quantity
Pizza dough 1 pound
Pizza sauce 1/3 cup
Mozzarella cheese 1 cup
Parmesan cheese 1/4 cup
Flour A small handful

Steps!

Step 1: Preheat your oven to 475°F. Lightly grease a baking sheet or dust it with a little flour.

Step 2: Shape the Dough

Step 3: Spread ⅓ cup of pizza sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border for the crust. Sprinkle 1 to 1.5 cups of shredded mozzarella cheese on top.

Step 4: Bake for 10 to 12 minutes until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly. Let it cool for 2 minutes, slice, and serve!
